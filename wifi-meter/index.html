What this measures

Web pages are not allowed to read the phone's radio signal level (the dBm figure), so this is not that number. It measures what the signal does to your connection instead: several times a second the page times a tiny request, and every second or two it times a short download. A weaker signal shows up as lower speed, slower and less steady responses, and dropped requests. The score combines those.

Turn off mobile data while you walk around, otherwise the phone can quietly switch to cellular where WiFi is weak and the reading will look better than it is. The downloads use up to about 25 MB of data per minute on a fast connection.
