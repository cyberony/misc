The word artificial

We already know
how to make
fake things.

Tools, forgeries, automata, software. “Artificial” is the boring half of the phrase.

The word intelligence

This half is
an argument,
not a fact.

We do not have a clean test for it. We have competing stories: learning, reasoning, language, free will, consciousness, survival.

08

Before code

  • ~400 BCThe Greeks imagine Talos, a bronze guardian, and Pandora, a made woman.
  • 1495da Vinci sketches a mechanical knight.
  • 1770The Turk: a chess “automaton” that was a man in a box. The hunger was real. The machine was a lie.
  • 1920Čapek’s play gives us the word robot.
09

The field gets a name

  • 1950Turing asks whether machines can think — and proposes a test instead of a metaphysics.
  • 1956Dartmouth. Ten people plan a six-week workshop. McCarthy coins “artificial intelligence.” That summer is still going.
  • early yearsProofs, ELIZA, SHRDLU, Shakey. Then the wall: a 50-step proof with 5 choices per step is 550 paths. You do not brute-force that.
10

Why this course exists

Search. Planning.
Representation.
Because the naive
tree is infinite.

The combinatorial explosion is not a trivia fact. It is why intelligence — biological or artificial — cannot be “try everything.”

11

Winters

  • 1958–69The perceptron is sold as a mind. Minsky and Papert show it is a linear classifier. It cannot even do XOR. Funding cools.
  • 1980sExpert systems: hand-built rules, expensive to grow, brittle to keep. Another thaw, then another freeze.
  • the pointThe work did not stop. The press release did.
12

The turn

Classical AI tried to teach a computer how to do a thing.

Machine learning tries to endow it with the capacity to learn the thing from data.

Neither cancelled the other. We will do both.

13

1997

Deep Blue beats Kasparov. A very smart machine at a very small game — not a person in a box.

14

The last twenty-five years

  • compute × data × algorithmsThat product, not a single insight, is what made machine learning loud.
  • 2012–Neural nets get rebranded as deep learning and then actually work at scale.
  • 2022ChatGPT. An inflection in how the public meets the field — not the end of the field.
15

Why you are here

To take intelligence seriously as a phenomenon, not a product category.
To look at the human mind without pretending we already understand it.
To build systems that do something useful, on purpose.
To sit with the implications — technical, economic, ethical, moral — before they sit with us.

17

Three words people throw around

Weak AI

Narrow. Good at a job. Most of what we will build, and most of what ships.

Strong AI / AGI

Human-level, general. A research horizon and a marketing term. Keep the two unconfused.

Superintelligence

Beyond us. Worth reading about. Not this quarter’s homework.
